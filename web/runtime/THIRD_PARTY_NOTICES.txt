Cinematic footage: scene transitions and backdrop loops in web/cinematic/media/
were generated with MiniMax H3 from this project's original script; task ids,
prompts and costs are recorded in web/cinematic/media/manifest.json and README.md.
Cinematic audio: voice, ambience and music in web/cinematic/audio/seed/ were
generated with Bytedance Seed Audio 1.0 (Volcengine and fal); prompts, costs and
hashes are recorded in manifest.json there. Use of generated media follows the
terms of the generating accounts.
Fallbacks drawn and synthesized by this project's own code (stage.js, sound.js)
are used when a clip or recording is missing or fails to load; subtitles, sound
captions and the notebook still carry every clue.
